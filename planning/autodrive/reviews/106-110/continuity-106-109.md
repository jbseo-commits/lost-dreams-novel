## Required changes

### Episode 106 — address form

At line 3 Seo-in asks Do-yun, `“도윤네 집이 기준계열이랑 관련 있어요?”` The confirmed address rule in `settings/04-protagonist-and-plot.md` requires Seo-in to call him `도윤` or `도윤 씨` and use polite speech; the unhonorific `도윤네` breaks that established form in direct address. Change the address while retaining the question’s meaning.

The Sunday sequence after 105, the family-review alert, the new family identities after the deliberately limited reveal in 105, and Garam’s female/early-20s/name-only presentation are consistent with the available approved material. The succession-review details (three-year cycle, blank joint-holder field, and family review procedure) are new proposed episode facts, not conflicts with established settings; keep them marked as proposals and do not treat them as confirmed general law.

### Episode 109 — disclose less about the ad-dream compute secret

At lines 47 and 73–79, Se-a describes the existing network as “밤마다 사람 머리를 빌려 쓰는 구조,” says the design would work “사람이 밤에 자기 머리를 내주지 않아도,” and contrasts it with a system that “사람을 안 갈아 넣으려면.” This exposes the sleeping-human cognition/brain-compute mechanism as known fact to Seo-in and the reader. `settings/03-dreams.md` §“작중 공개 범위” (lines 7–9) explicitly keeps that mechanism secret, says even limited operators must distinguish observed allocation/delay from the still-hidden source, and forbids widening the disclosure timing or character scope without a separate decision. The 106–110 plan also reserves the full mechanism and its connection to memory replay for later.

Keep Se-a’s proposal about expensive machine computation and voluntary daytime reference pools, but describe the problem through already observable service or supply constraints. Do not state or imply the ad-dream network’s actual sleeping-brain computation mechanism as an established fact in this scene.

### Episode 109 — make the proposal’s transmission path explicit or remove the exact quotation

At lines 53–59, Se-a says she saw Seo-in’s exact early-return restriction in a handout circulated by So-hyeon. Episode 105 lines 113–115 records the reviewed bundle being sent to the existing reviewers and explicitly says no new recipient was added; the proposal remains `HOLD / CONSENT CONFLICT`. Episodes 106–108 do not establish So-hyeon sharing this exact clause with Se-a or the technical institute. Episode 109 therefore gives Se-a knowledge of the verbatim held draft without an established, consented transfer path. The visibility gate requires a traceable document/recipient path.

Either establish, in the current scene, the specific authorized transmission and the limited material Se-a received, or remove the verbatim knowledge and let her respond to the technical inquiry and the condition actually sent to her supervisor. Do not imply that the held proposal entered a wider review or was approved.
